# V3.0 — 真人驱动的素材叙事

Activate on “按照 v3.0 剪辑” and explicit v3.0 equivalents; persist `editMode: narrative-v3`. This is a separate narrative mode, not a new black-gold palette or automatic replacement for classic/premium-v2.

## Editorial intent

The presenter makes the argument, footage supplies concrete situations, diagrams explain relationships, and short concept typography creates memory anchors. Alternate by meaning, not a fixed cut interval or presenter percentage. Do not force every sentence into a card or build a continuous slideshow. Start with the established moving full-frame presenter/title opening; retain the existing matching-cover workflow.

For each beat, choose what the audience needs:

| Goal | Preferred treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Believe a specific fact | Verified original footage, product operation, source page or evidence image |
| Understand a relationship | Comparison, causal chain, process or quantity diagram |
| Feel a situation | Relevant real action or situational illustration |
| Remember a concept | Short large phrase, chapter title or recurring graphic motif |
| Follow a judgment | Full-frame presenter, expression and natural delivery |

## Plan before sourcing

After ChatCut cleanup and timing freeze, annotate each EDL scene with `viewerGoal`, `presentation` (speaker / footage / diagram / hybrid), `beatReason`, and any `conceptText`. For footage needs add subject, visible action, setting, intended emotion when relevant, and `footageRole` (object / example / emotion / metaphor). Separately record evidence versus illustration; an emotional match is not factual proof.

Use the existing source ledger and [B-roll sourcing](broll-sourcing.md): plan needs → ChatCut `search_stock_media` → inspect actual clips and provenance → download and verify → select source in/out → HyperFrames packaging → Remotion placement. Preserve stable IDs and absolute frozen speech timings. Plan promising searches rather than requiring a fixed number of accepted clips. If search fails, record the reason and choose presenter or a simple diagram; never pad with unrelated stock.

Search concrete visible actions rather than abstract nouns alone. For decision overload, for example, seek a person comparing alternatives and hesitating, not a generic city clip tagged “life”. Specific product claims require matching product/version/context footage; generic footage is illustration only. Prefer supplied/approved assets and authorized real footage. No automatic film/TV downloads, copied creator assets, paid generation, or new tool installation. Preserve rights checks and required credits.

## Presentation and human rhythm

V3.0 allows full-frame footage, floating clips, simple diagrams, and full-frame presenter. Unlike classic, it may replace the visible presenter during an illustrative beat while the frozen dialogue continues. Unlike premium-v2, it does not require a continuous diagram world or a circular presenter on every explanation.

Return to the full-frame presenter for questions, interpretation, judgments, concept handoffs, advice and closure. PiP is optional when expression or continuity adds value; omit it when it distracts or obscures evidence. Record the choice in `beatReason`. If circular PiP is used, it must show the synchronized actual speaker with muted duplicate audio; localized masking is permitted only for that PiP, never the source master or full-frame returns.

Maintain source framing, ratio, natural skin color and lip sync. Do not infer permission to crop/punch in or invent camera angles from reference videos. Select suitable supplied angles only when available. Keep source footage proportions within cards/scenes; do not stretch to fill a frame. Keep story objects and visual identity continuous across related beats.

## Text and visual grammar

Use three distinct layers: readable ordinary captions for speech, short concept typography for memory, and labeled diagrams for explanation. Do not display the same sentence in all three. Large words summarize the idea rather than copy the caption; retain a separate clear caption lane and one dominant reading target.

Choose fonts actually installed and licensed, with a consistent display/body hierarchy saved in DESIGN.md. Use the house semantic colors as a starting point, not a mandatory gold/serif imitation. Do not identify a reference's exact font or production software without evidence. Keep product interfaces and evidence naturally readable; do not globally darken/recolor them for stylistic uniformity.

Reveal meaningful units in order: concept then contrast, object then relation, baseline then difference. Hold for reading, then clear. Long multi-concept pieces may use a concise overview and chapter markers; short single-argument videos need no forced directory or chapter count. Do not copy the references' watermark, artwork, exact layouts, decorative English labels or large letter spacing as a required signature.

HyperFrames owns visual framing, titles and internal animation; Remotion places footage and assets from the shared EDL. Keep source trim separate from timeline placement, avoid double-baked trims, and retain one dialogue stream. Stock audio is muted unless deliberately selected and mixed. Restrained semantic SFX are optional; do not claim the reference's sound design was verified or add music/paid generation by implication.

## Acceptance

- Mode is narrative-v3 in DESIGN.md/EDL; classic and premium-v2 defaults remain separate.
- Every inserted clip has a spoken anchor, visible purpose, actual-frame review and source/rights record; no illustrative clip poses as evidence.
- Presenter returns support argument or interpretation; there is no mechanical timer or mandatory PiP everywhere.
- Caption, concept and diagram layers do different jobs and remain readable in final output.
- Chapter complexity fits duration; footage/diagrams do not overwhelm short speech.
- Shared quality checklist passes, including original framing, source-grade detail, natural color, timing, single audio stream and matching cover.
- Inspect actual composed footage entries/exits, important actions, diagrams and presenter returns. Include broll-manifest.json and required credits when footage is used.
